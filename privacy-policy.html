Laaj Studios Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Laaj Studios provides wedding photography and cinematography services. This policy explains how we handle information supplied through our website, Meta Instant Forms, and related wedding inquiries.

Information we collect

When you inquire, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, wedding date, venue or city, requested services, and additional event details. Please do not include payment details, identity documents, or other sensitive information in an inquiry form.

Our website hosting and form providers may process technical information needed to deliver their services, such as your IP address, browser information, and request logs. Inquiry links may include campaign identifiers that help us understand how people find our services.

How we use information

We use inquiry information to respond to your request, check availability, discuss your celebration, prepare proposals, and manage related communications. Submitting an inquiry does not reserve a date or create a booking.

How information is shared

We use service providers to host our website, deliver forms and messages, and manage inquiries. They may process information as needed to provide these services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect legal rights. We do not sell inquiry contact information.

If you submit a form through Facebook or Instagram, Meta also processes information under its own privacy policy. If you contact us through WhatsApp or another third-party platform, that platform's privacy terms also apply.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry information as needed to respond, provide requested services, maintain relevant business records, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, but no electronic service can guarantee complete security.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your inquiry information, or stop further promotional contact, by emailing photo@laajstudios.co. We may need to verify your request and retain information where required for legal or contractual reasons. You can choose not to provide optional event details.

Children

Our inquiry forms are intended for adults arranging photography and cinematography services. Please contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information that should be removed.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy by posting a revised version with a new effective date. For questions about your information, contact Laaj Studios at photo@laajstudios.co or visit laajstudios.co.
