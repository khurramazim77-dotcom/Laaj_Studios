Questions

Before you enquire

How far in advance should we book?

Most of our couples reserve 9 to 14 months ahead, and peak-season Saturdays go first. If your date is sooner, ask anyway; we keep a few dates open for short-notice celebrations.

Do you cover multi-day weddings?

Yes, it is most of what we do. Dholki, mehndi, nikkah, baraat, walima and reception can be covered individually or together as one multi-day booking.

Do you travel?

Yes, wherever your celebration takes place. Any travel is quoted at cost in your proposal.

Can we have both photography and film?

We recommend it. One studio means one team that plans together and never gets in each other's frames. Booking both together is priced below hiring two separate vendors.

When will we receive our photos and films?

A sneak peek within 72 hours, the full gallery within 6 to 8 weeks, and films within 10 to 12 weeks. Albums are designed with you once the gallery is delivered.

Do you photograph modest or segregated events?

Yes. We can provide women photographers and cinematographers for ladies-only events and are always respectful of family and religious preferences.
